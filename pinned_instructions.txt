**📌 How to use the Prediction Bot (Discord)**

This bot lets everyone log their **leverage calls** and tracks who’s actually profitable with a transparent +1 / -1 scoring system.

### Log a Prediction
Use the slash command:
```
/predict asset direction entry_price leverage notes
```

**Examples:**
- `/predict BTC short 98000 10`
- `/predict ETH long 2450 5 "Breakout retest"`
- `/predict SOL short 145 20 "Fakeout incoming"`

The bot will post a nice embed with your call + a unique ID (e.g. **#42**).

### Resolve / Close Your Call
When the move plays out:
```
/resolve ID win     → successful (+1 to your score)
/resolve ID loss    → didn’t work (-1 to your score)
```

**Example:** `/resolve 42 win`

Only **you** or admins can resolve your calls.

### Check Performance
- `/stats` → Your personal wins, losses, score & win rate
- `/leaderboard` → Top performers in the server
- `/active` → See all open (unresolved) calls right now
- `/help` → Quick reminder of commands

### Quick Rules
- Be honest when resolving your own calls
- You can only resolve calls **you** posted (admins can help)
- Use the notes field to explain your idea
- Have fun and try to stay green on the leaderboard 🟢

Need help? Just use `/help` or ask in the server.
